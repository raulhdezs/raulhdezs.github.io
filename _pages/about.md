<!--### Bio-->

Raúl Hernández Sánchez, an Assistant Professor at the [University Carlos III of Madrid (UC3M)](https://www.uc3m.es).
I studied Industrial Engineering at UC3M, where I completed both my [BSc](https://www.uc3m.es/bachelor-degree/industrial-technologies) and [MSc](https://www.uc3m.es/master/industrial-engineering), and started exploring shock wave ignition with [Prof. César Huete](http://fluidosuc3m.es/people/chuete/).

After that, I moved to Marseille for my [PhD in Mechanics and Physics of Fluids](https://ecole-doctorale-353.univ-amu.fr/) at [Aix-Marseille University](https://www.univ-amu.fr/) studying detonation initiation and deflagration-to-detonation transition with [Prof. Bruno Denet](https://www.irphe.fr/~denet/) and [Prof. Paul Clavin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Clavin) at [IRPHE](https://irphe.fr/). 

Now back at UC3M, I work on experiments with hydrogen/ammonia/air flames with [Prof. Mario Sánchez](http://fluidosuc3m.es/people/mssanz/), and on modelling and studying spray detonations with [Prof. César Huete](http://fluidosuc3m.es/people/chuete/) and [Prof. Daniel Martínez](https://blogs.upm.es/labcmf/people/#)

### [Research](/research/)

My research explores unsteady combustion phenomena that play a critical role in the dafe use of carbon-free fuels. I combine theory, numerical analysis and experiments to better understand these processes and support the transition to cleaner energy systems.

### [Teaching](/teaching/)

I have taught a wide range of subjects in Fluid Mechanics and Energy Engineering at different levels and institutions.
During my PhD at IRPHE, I had the opportunity to teach at [Polytech Marseille](https://polytech.univ-amu.fr/fr/formations/cycle-ingenieur/mecanique-energetique) and [IUT Aix-Marseille Université](https://iut.univ-amu.fr/fr/formations/bachelor-universitaire-de-technologie/but-metiers-transition-efficacite-energetiques), delivering courses in energy and mechanics.
Currently at UC3M, I teach undergraduate and master's courses in [Engineering Fluid Mechanics](https://aplicaciones.uc3m.es/cpa/generaFicha?est=256&plan=418&asig=15499&idioma=2), [Fluid Mechanics](https://aplicaciones.uc3m.es/cpa/generaFicha?est=251&plan=421&asig=14170&idioma=2) and [Advanced topics in fluid mechanics](https://aplicaciones.uc3m.es/cpa/generaFicha?est=265&asig=16158&idioma=2) across BSc programs of [Mechanics](https://www.uc3m.es/bachelor-degree/mechanical), and [Industrial Engineering](https://www.uc3m.es/bachelor-degree/industrial-technologies), [Aerospace Engineering](https://www.uc3m.es/bachelor-degree/aeroespace), and the [MSc in Industrial Mechanics](https://www.uc3m.es/master/industrial-mechanics), while also supervising Bachelor and Master's theses.
